Managing your billing.

You can look after your Tropesmith payments yourself, any time, from your library. Here is where to look.

Where to manage your billing

  1. Sign in with the email you used to buy. We send you a one-time link; there is no password.
  2. Open your library.
  3. Scroll to the Billing section. If you have a monthly plan, the Manage subscription button is there, and also at the top of the page.

The button opens a secure page run by Stripe, our payment provider. You do not need to give us any card details on our own site.

Update your card

  1. In your library, choose Manage subscription.
  2. On the Stripe page, open the payment methods area and add your new card.
  3. Make the new card your default, then remove the old one if you like.

If a payment did not go through, updating your card here is the way to fix it. The exact wording on the Stripe page may differ a little from the steps above.

See your next charge

Choose Manage subscription in your library. The Stripe page shows your plan, what it costs and the date of your next charge.

Past invoices and receipts

Monthly plan. Choose Manage subscription. The Stripe page keeps your invoice history, and you can open or download any invoice from there.

Credit packs and Maps. These are one-time purchases, so they are not on the subscription page. Instead, open the Billing section of your library. It lists your paid purchases with the date, what you bought and the amount. Choose View receipt beside a purchase to open its receipt. Each monthly charge is listed there too, with a View invoice link. The newest 25 orders show first. If you have more, choose Show older orders under the list to see the next 25, and repeat until the button is gone. An order that cost nothing, such as one paid in full with a coupon, is listed as No charge and has no link.

Stripe also emails a receipt to the address you used at checkout when you buy.

A purchase we added for you by hand is listed without a receipt link. If you need a receipt for one of those, email support@tropesmith.com and we will send one.

Cancel the monthly plan

Renews monthly until you cancel; cancel any time from your library.

  1. In your library, choose Manage subscription.
  2. On the Stripe page, choose to cancel your plan.
  3. Check the date shown on the confirmation, then confirm.

Cancelling stops future monthly charges. The Stripe page tells you what happens to the plan you have already paid for before you confirm.

More than one plan

If you have more than one monthly plan, Manage subscription opens the plan that needs attention first (for example, one with a failed payment), otherwise your newest one. If your plans were bought with different emails, the button may open only one of them. Email support@tropesmith.com and we will help with the other.

Still stuck?

Email support@tropesmith.com from the address you used to buy, and tell us what you are trying to do. Go back to your library or pricing.
