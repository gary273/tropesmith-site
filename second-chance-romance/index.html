Each signal gets a rarity tag (common, underserved or novel) from the text of that review or post alone; the classifier is never shown what is on the shelf. A tag points at where to look. What the Second Chance Romance trope promises

Second chance romance reunites former lovers and asks whether the thing that broke them can be mended. The history is the hook: the leads already know each other’s worst, so every scene carries the weight of what was lost and the risk of losing it twice. Regret, growth and hard-won trust do the emotional lifting.

It is a trope that depends on the author selling the reunion. 36% of the demand signals that mention second chance romance are tagged underserved or novel by Tropesmith's classifier, compared with 39% across all demand signals.
