What "Hard Magic System" promises readers

A hard magic system gives its rules to the reader up front and keeps them fixed — costs, limits and mechanics that stay consistent, so the climax can be won by a character using the rules cleverly rather than the plot bending to let them win. It's a promise to a specific kind of fantasy reader: one who wants to understand the tool before it's used.

It's a smaller absolute number than the romance-side tropes on this page, which is a genuine artifact of TropeSmith's extraction policy rather than a market fact — the spine currently prioritizes romance extraction, so fantasy demand is real but under-measured relative to how it would look with equal extraction depth. Treat the number as a floor, not a ceiling.

How to use this before you draft

Read the three numbers above together, not separately. 16,877 requests tells you the trope is wanted at real scale; 100% underserved tells you how much of that want has no matching tagged supply yet; and the 11% recent-request share tells you whether that gap is closing or holding steady right now. A high request count with low underserved means the trope is proven but the shelf has largely caught up — the opportunity is in a specific unclaimed angle, not the trope's bare existence. A high underserved figure means more room, but pair it with the rejection data above: readers punish weak execution of a trope they want just as fast as they ignore one they don't.
