What "reader demand" means here

Tropesmith reads the romance market continuously — the tropes readers name on BookTok, the shelves they build on Goodreads, and the books they ask for in reader communities. Each subgenre page separates the tropes that are simply popular (often the same as saturated) from the ones quietly gaining momentum, where a new book still has room to land. It's a thin slice of what a full Tropesmith Map does for one specific book — the ranked trope stack, comps, cover and title direction, pricing, and a pre-draft plan.

Why write toward demand you can prove

Chasing the loudest trend usually means adding to a pile. A book that lands in a high-demand, low-supply pocket does marketing a great-but-crowded book can't do for itself. Start with your lane above, or build a full Map for your exact book.
