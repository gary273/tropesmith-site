The gap readers keep naming

These are the loudest under-filled asks in dark romance right now — high, repeated reader demand meeting thin supply. Write here and you're writing toward demand you can prove.

Loudest · underserved

The masked, villain-coded hero

Ghostface-and-slasher-coded love interests — dangerous, masked, mysterious. Readers name this one over and over and can't find enough of it. The strongest single unmet signal in the lane.

Rising · room to own

Motorcycle-club dark romance

Fastest-climbing setting in the lane and still not crowded. The pack, the loyalty, the outlaw code — demand is outrunning what's on the shelf.

Rising · thin supply

Pack dynamics & breeding kink

Two of the fastest-rising spice-and-structure signals in dark romance, both still underserved. Readers are asking; few books are answering.

Underserved · atmosphere

Dark fantasy, castle & court

Heavy-atmosphere dark fantasy romance — medieval settings, courts, high stakes. Consistent reader demand, not enough books hitting the mood.

Why "rising" beats "popular" in dark romance

Dark romance is one of the most crowded corners in indie publishing, which is exactly why chasing the loudest trope usually means adding to a pile. A book that lands in a high-demand, low-supply pocket — the masked villain-coded hero, the outlaw MC world, the underserved spice structures — does marketing a great-but-crowded book can't do for itself.
