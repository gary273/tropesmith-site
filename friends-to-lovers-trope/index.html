What the Friends to Lovers trope promises

Friends to lovers turns established intimacy into romantic risk. The leads already know each other — the history, the inside jokes, the trust — so the tension is not whether they are compatible but whether they will gamble the friendship on something more. The fear of losing what they already have is the whole engine.

It reads warm and low-angst on the surface but carries real stakes underneath, which is why it is a reliably requested character dynamic. It is a natural slow burn and pairs with grumpy-sunshine and second chance. The underserved share marks a steady, affection-forward lane that stays open for authors who sell the tip from friend to more.

How to read this

A high request count tells you friends to lovers is wanted. A high underserved figure tells you the shelf hasn't caught up. When both are high, that's demand the market hasn't answered yet — an open lane rather than a crowded one.
