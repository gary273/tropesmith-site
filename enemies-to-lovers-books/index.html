What counts as an enemies to lovers book

The trope is simple to state: the leads begin against each other, and the relationship the book is about grows out of that opposition. The rivalry can be professional, political, personal or a feud between families or factions. What makes it enemies to lovers, and not just a slow burn, is that readers describe the opposition itself as the thing they came for.

So we did not pick books by cover copy or our own reading. A title is on this page only if readers tagged it and named the trope in their reviews. How that check works is under how we checked every title.

A demand signal is one specific thing a reader asked for or praised in a review, comment or post, taken from Goodreads reviews, Amazon reviews, BookTok comments and captions, Reddit posts and Amazon Q&A, and tagged by trope. One review can give several. Each signal gets a rarity tag (common, underserved or novel) from the text of that review or post alone; the classifier is never shown what is on the shelf. A tag points at where to look; it does not prove that no book answers the signal. How we count.

How to read this

A trope can be common and still leave room: the share of its demand signals tagged underserved or novel shows how often readers ask for something specific that they feel they are not getting. Compare it with the 39% across all demand signals, then check the comp titles in your own lane.

How we checked every title

A title is listed only if all three of these hold:

  • The title and author are in our book tables.
  • At least five reader reviews we hold name enemies to lovers as a trope of that book.
  • A second source agrees: the Goodreads Enemies To Lovers shelf label, a mention in the book description, or a match in our book-trope data.

Titles with review mentions only were left out. We added no title from memory, and the year shown is the publication year Goodreads holds, which can reflect a later edition.

What to keep in mind. The review counts are small (five to a few dozen per book) because they come from the review sample we hold, not from every review ever written; they show that readers name the trope, not how many readers do. The Goodreads label is a reader-made shelf, not an editor’s judgment. Some well-known titles are missing because they did not clear the bar in our data, and foreign-language edition rows were excluded.

FAQ

What is an enemies to lovers book?

An enemies to lovers book is a romance in which the two leads start as opponents, rivals or people with a real reason to dislike each other, and the story turns that friction into attraction. Readers shelve and search by the trope, so on this page a book counts only if our data shows readers naming it.

How common is enemies to lovers in reader data?

Enemies to lovers is in 2.1% of reader-review signals and 6.4% of TikTok comment signals. 32% of the demand signals that mention it are tagged underserved or novel by Tropesmith's classifier, compared with 39% across all demand signals. Counted 16 October 2026, 02:40 UTC. A demand signal is one specific thing a reader asked for or praised in a review, comment or post.

Which kinds of romance have the most enemies to lovers books on this list?

Of the 36 books that passed our check, 11 are romantasy, 11 contemporary, 7 dark, 5 sports and 2 historical. The mix reflects the books we hold data on, not the whole shelf.

How did you choose these books?

Each title had to be in our book tables, be named as enemies to lovers in at least five reader reviews we hold, and have a second source: the Goodreads Enemies To Lovers shelf label, a mention in the book description, or a match in our book-trope data. Titles with review mentions only were left out.
