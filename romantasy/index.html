What is romantasy?

Romantasy is romance blended with fantasy: a love story at the centre of a fantasy world, so the romance arc and the stakes of the world drive each other. Readers shop for it as its own genre, with its own tropes, such as fated mates and found family.

Where do romantasy tropes sit among other romance tropes?

The romance tropes list ranks the main romance tropes by their share of all demand signals and of TikTok comment signals, and links each one to its own page. Romantasy favourites such as found family, fated mates and enemies to lovers appear on both pages.
