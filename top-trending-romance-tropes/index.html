Found Family leads the romance board at 267,433 requests. By contrast, Series Continuation closes out this ranking at 32,159 requests — still enough real reader demand to track, just a smaller slice of a corpus this size. The gap between rank #1 and rank #12 is one reason raw request count alone is a starting point, not a verdict: a mid-ranked trope with a high 14-day share can be moving faster right now than a bigger trope that's plateaued.

The median request count across these 12 tropes is 48,286, with 6 of 12 sitting at or above it — a reminder that this list isn't evenly spread from top to bottom, it's front-loaded toward a handful of high-volume tropes with a longer tail behind them. 1 of the 12 tropes here are logging 15% or more of their all-time volume in just the last 14 days (Historical Fiction) — worth a second look even if they aren't the biggest names on the board.

Raw request count is what this page ranks by, but Tropesmith also tracks a weighted-strength score per trope that blends recency and cross-source corroboration rather than counting every mention the same. By that measure Found Family still comes out on top (884,023 weighted strength against its 267,433 raw requests, a 3.31x ratio). The average weighted-to-raw ratio across the 12 tropes on this page is 3.21x; Found Family's 3.31x sits above that average.
