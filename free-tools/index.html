Put our data on your site

The Live Board is embeddable, free, and free to use commercially. Paste this where you want the card:

<div id="tropesmith-live-board"></div>
<script src="https://tropesmith.com/embed/live-board.js?lane=romance.dark" async></script>

The only condition is attribution: keep the visible “Data: Tropesmith” credit link back to tropesmith.com. Do not strip it, hide it or nofollow it — that link is what pays for the data. Full options, themes and the iframe version are on the embed page.

Where these numbers come from

Counted, not estimated: 3,950,000+ Goodreads reviews, 2,810,000+ shelf signals, 2,590,000+ demand signals and 122,000+ BookTok videos across 710+ tracked hashtags. Across reviews, shelf signals, demand signals and BookTok videos, 9,290,000+ records have been analyzed (a demand signal is drawn from a review or post, so the reviews and the demand signals drawn from them are both counted). The registry holds 450,000+ titles, 105,000+ of them tagged trope by trope across 5,070+ distinct tropes and 140+ demand lanes. Nothing on this page is a guess or a round number typed in by hand. The demand signals figure was counted 2026-10-07 17:24 UTC; every other figure was counted 2026-09-25 09:42 UTC. A demand signal is one specific thing a reader asked for or praised in a review, comment or post, picked out by Tropesmith's classifier. One review can give several. How we count
