What "Protective Hero" promises readers

The protective hero puts a love interest's safety ahead of almost everything else in the plot — bodyguard-adjacent, willing to burn the world down rather than lose the person they've claimed. In fantasy specifically it usually pairs with power asymmetry: the hero can protect because they're dangerous, and the tension is whether that danger ever turns inward.

It's the top fantasy-genre trope by raw request volume in Tropesmith's corpus, which matters because fantasy overall is thinner-covered ground for this engine than romance — see the note on genre coverage below. A trope this requested, in a genre this under-mapped, is worth more attention than the raw numbers alone suggest.
