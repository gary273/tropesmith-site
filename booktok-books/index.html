What is a BookTok book?

A BookTok book is a title that TikTok's book community recommends, reviews or reacts to in videos, usually under tags such as #booktok. This is not an official TikTok list, and lists differ in how they count. Two common ways are below.

  • A voted list. The Goodreads list titled BookTok held 143 books on 17 October 2026, and its own header says a book's score is based on multiple factors, including how many people voted for it and how highly they ranked it.
  • A count of mentions. This page counts how many BookTok videos in our scan name each title, and shows the tropes we have on file for it.

A voted list tells you which books readers like. A mention count tells you what BookTok keeps talking about. Neither tells you what will sell.

What do the most-named BookTok books have in common?

The three fantasy titles with tropes on file all carry heroine-under-pressure tags, and two add a powerful or dangerous love interest. Fourth Wing carries academy romance, alpha MMC and broken FMC. A Court of Thorns and Roses carries beast-tamer, beauty-and-the-beast and captive/captor. Throne of Glass carries assassin FMC, badass heroine and captivity.

That is three books, not the whole table, and only some titles have tropes on file, so it is a reason to look closer at those tropes, not proof that a book built from them will sell.

Does a BookTok book sell, and should I write what BookTok names?

A title named in many videos shows conversation, not purchases, so it does not tell you whether a new book will sell. What it does show is which tropes readers are drawn to, and the next question is whether the shelf already answers them. A loud trope that is already well supplied puts your book into a queue, not into an opening.

Frequently asked questions

What are BookTok books?

BookTok books are the titles TikTok's book community recommends, reviews or reacts to in videos. Lists differ in how they count, and this page is not an official TikTok list. Tropesmith counts the titles named in BookTok videos in its scan and shows the tropes we have on file for each.

What is the most popular BookTok book?

In Tropesmith's scan of BookTok videos from 11 May to 21 August 2026, the title named in the most videos was Fourth Wing by Rebecca Yarros, followed by A Court of Thorns and Roses by Sarah J. Maas, after the page's filters remove titles that are also ordinary phrases or that are spread thinly across many unrelated hashtags. This counts videos that name a title. It is not sales data or an official TikTok chart.

Do BookTok books actually sell?

A title can be named in many videos and still sell modestly, and the reverse is also true. Mentions measure conversation, not purchases. Use BookTok titles to find tropes readers are drawn to, then check whether the shelf already supplies them.

How are the titles on this page chosen?

We match book titles against the text of BookTok videos in our scan and count the videos that name each one. Matching is automated, and the page leaves out titles that are also ordinary phrases and titles spread thinly across many unrelated hashtags, but a wrong match can still get through. Treat the list as a lead to check, not a verdict.

How current is this list?

This list is a snapshot dated 21 August 2026, and the date shows at the top of the page. If a list like this looks old, treat it as old.

Should I write what is trending on BookTok?

Not on that signal alone. A trope that is loud on BookTok and already well supplied on the shelf puts a new book into competition. Check demand against supply for your own genre first, using the free Trope Demand Checker and Trope Pair Finder.
