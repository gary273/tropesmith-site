Frequently asked questions

What are BookTok books?

BookTok books are the titles and tropes gaining traction on TikTok's #BookTok community. Tropesmith tracks 489 BookTok book hashtags on an hourly scan, so the activity here reflects what readers are posting about right now, not a static list.

What is trending on BookTok right now?

As of our latest pass on 2026-08-14, the most active book hashtags in our scan include #newadultromance, #booktok, #fantasybooktok, #romanticsuspense, #horrorbooktok, #romcombooks. Each is a live count of recent videos our scanner logged for that tag.

Do BookTok trends match what readers actually want?

Often, but not always. Tropesmith cross-checks BookTok activity against measured reader demand: a tag can be loud on BookTok while the trope behind it stays underserved on the shelf. The map on this page pairs each BookTok hashtag with its reader-request count and underserved share so you can see both signals side by side.
