The number that actually matters

Hashtag volume tells you what readers are talking about. It does not tell you where the talk outruns the supply. A trope can be everywhere on BookTok and already answered by two hundred books — publishing into that is competition, not opportunity. Frequently asked questions

What are BookTok books?

BookTok books are the titles TikTok's book community is recommending at a given moment. They are not an official list: BookTok is a live conversation, so the set changes weekly. Tropesmith identifies them by scanning 620+ book hashtags every hour, logging the videos, and matching the titles named in them against a registry of 290,000+ books. Across the current window (2026-05-11 to 2026-08-21) that produced 567 title mentions across 1,662 videos. The most-mentioned title right now is Fourth Wing by Rebecca Yarros.

What BookTok book is trending right now?

In our latest pass (scan of 2026-08-21), the titles named in the most BookTok videos are Fourth Wing by Rebecca Yarros (74 videos) and A Court of Thorns and Roses by Sarah J. Maas (49 videos). This is a mention count from our own scan of 620+ tracked hashtags, not a sales figure and not an official TikTok chart - a book can be mentioned heavily and still sell modestly, and vice versa.

What tropes does BookTok ask for most?

Ranked by reader requests read from TikTok videos and comments, the top three are Slow burn, Found family, Enemies to lovers. A reader request is one video or comment where a reader describes a book they want but cannot find. We have classified 32,655 such requests from BookTok, out of 824,276 tracked across all sources.

Do BookTok trends actually mean a book will sell?

No, and it is worth being blunt about it. Hashtag volume measures conversation, not purchase. A trope can be loud on BookTok and already well supplied on the shelf, in which case a new book lands into competition rather than into a gap. The number that matters to an author is the underserved share: how much of the demand our engine flags as unmet by books currently available. That is the column on this page that a hashtag counter cannot give you.

How often is this page updated?

The scanner runs hourly across 620+ hashtags; the latest pass reflected here is 2026-08-21. The tables are regenerated from the corpus, and every figure carries the date it was counted. If a number on this page is not dated, treat it as wrong and tell us.

How should I approach booktok books?

Treat BookTok as a demand signal, not a bestseller list. Tropesmith scans 620+ book hashtags and 89,700+ videos, and the most useful thing in that data is not which titles are trending — it is which tropes readers keep asking for. Start from the request, check whether the shelf already supplies it, and write to the gap rather than to the hashtag: a loud trope that is already well supplied puts your book into a queue, not into an opening. Every figure on this page carries the date it was counted; the latest pass was 2026-08-21.

How should I approach book lovers as an audience on BookTok?

Listen to how book lovers describe what they want, and use their words. Tropesmith reads reader requests out of BookTok videos and comments — one request is one reader describing a book they want and cannot find — and ranks tropes by how often that happens. Ranked that way, the top three are Slow burn, Found family, Enemies to lovers. Readers on BookTok rarely describe a book by genre or category; they describe it by trope, so the phrases they use are also the keywords, the comp language and the blurb vocabulary your listing should be speaking.
