Is Dark Romance Oversaturated?

Written 9 October 2026 · Figures counted 9 October 2026 · Source: Tropesmith reader-demand engine

Our data cannot show that dark romance is oversaturated. On the reader side, dark romance has stayed between 2.8% and 4.4% of romance demand signals in every quarter since 2024, which does not look like readers moving on, though its share has eased a little in Goodreads reviews. Oversaturation means too many books for those readers, and we do not count every book on the shelf, so we cannot give you a saturation number.

What would "oversaturated" have to mean?

It means the supply of books has outrun what readers want. That takes two measurements: how many books there are, and how much readers want them. Tropesmith's counts cover the second only in part: they come from what readers write in reviews, comments and posts. For the first it tracks bestseller charts and a registry of tagged titles, not every book on Amazon. Anyone who gives you a flat yes or no is guessing.

Across all sources the share ranged from 2.8% to 4.4% and sat between 3.5% and 3.9% in 2026. Within Goodreads reviews alone it rose from 2.6% to a peak of 3.8% in the last quarter of 2025 and has eased to 3.1% in the third quarter of 2026. BookTok comments mention dark romance at a much higher rate, between about 5% and 10% of their romance signals, and are a larger slice of the corpus than in 2024 (7% of romance signals then, 11% in 2026 Q3), which helps keep the all-source figure steadier than the review figure. Tropesmith reads reviews some time after they are posted, so the newest quarter is the least complete. Treat this as a rough guide, not a trend line.

What should you check for your own book?

  1. Look at the lane. The dark romance market snapshot shows the supply side: competing titles, new chart entrants and the typical price.
  2. Check your core trope. Put it through the Trope Demand checker and compare it with its neighbors in the same genre.
  3. Read the comps. Recent reviews of three books you would sit beside show what readers praise and what they complain about.
  4. Narrow the promise. Dark romance stacks onto mafia, captive, stalker and anti-hero setups. A tighter mix is easier to stand out in than the whole lane. Test pairings with Trope Pairs.

Frequently asked questions

Is dark romance still popular in 2026?

Readers still mention it. Across all sources, dark romance appeared in 3.5% to 3.9% of romance demand signals in each quarter of 2026 so far, though its share in Goodreads reviews alone has eased since late 2025. Mentions are not sales, so use them to compare tropes with each other, not to forecast earnings.

Is dark romance too crowded for a new author?

Crowded and closed are different things. Our counts cover the reader side only, so check the supply side yourself: the market snapshot shows how many new titles recently entered the lane's bestseller charts. If new titles keep entering, new books are still finding readers. That is a sign, not proof.

What is the difference between popular and oversaturated?

Popular means many readers want it. Oversaturated means there are more books than those readers can absorb. A lane can be both popular and crowded, and the useful question for your book is whether your particular trope mix is crowded.
